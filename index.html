After the session

Write notes the way you always do. No recording needed.

Paste typed notes after the session. Nothing changes in your wiki until you accept it, and every fact keeps a link to the note it came from.

  1. Paste your notes

    Shorthand, typos, half-sentences. Names get matched to pages you already have.

  2. Review the proposed facts

    New facts for existing pages, new NPCs saved as drafts, and a question when something is ambiguous. You choose what's canon and who can see it.

  3. Prep the next session

    Loose threads, who's likely to show up, and what each of them wants, on one screen.

What's inside

Built for the DM who's twelve sessions in and losing track.

A wiki that links itself

NPCs, locations, factions, items, quests, and lore, connected with [[links]] the way you'd write them by hand.

Answers with sources

Ask about your world and every answer names the pages and sessions it came from. If it isn't in your notes, it says so.

Drafts stay drafts

Generated NPCs, encounters, and descriptions are marked as drafts until you make them canon.

Next-session prep

Open threads, NPCs likely to appear, and who owes whom, pulled together before game night.

No recording needed

Works from the notes you already take. Good for in-person tables and groups that don't want their sessions recorded.

Secrets, one fact at a time

Mark any single fact DM-only. It stays out of the player journal and out of any answer a player can see.

Your files

Your canon is plain markdown. Take it anywhere.

Every page is a markdown file that opens in Obsidian or any editor, with its facts and sources included. Export whenever you like. Leaving DM Companion never means losing your campaign.
